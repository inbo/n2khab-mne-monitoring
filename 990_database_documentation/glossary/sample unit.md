In the context of the #MNE #REP, a sample unit is uniquely defined by a **location** (identifier: usually the [[glossary/GRTS address|GRTS address]]) and a targeted **habitat** (i.e. [[glossary/habitat type and stratum|type or stratum]]).

Thus, the [[glossary/characteristic columns|characteristic columns]] of #SampleUnits are `grts_address` and `type`.
