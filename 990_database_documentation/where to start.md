In case you are completely new to the wonderful world of [[glossary/MNE|MNE]] databases, this might be a good guide to build knowledge.
*If we would ever write a book about our databases and information flows, these might be the chapters.*

+ [[server/server|technical setup]]: everything needed to understand the virtual hardware on which we host our databases
+ [[database/database|databases]] and [[database/mirrors|mirrors]]: all the bespoke back-ends and their virtual copies
+ [[database/userroles|userroles]]: who may access the data, and why
+ [[database/structure|structure]]: overview of the general structure of an MNM database
+ [[usage/usage|usage]]: how to access the database
+ [[database/backups|backups]]: when and how we safe-copy our data
+ [[maintenance/recurrent|daily maintenance scripts]]: recurrent tasks which enable data flow between databases
+ [[usage/usage|usage]]: different interfaces and entry points for effective usage of the database tooling


To deepen your knowledge about plain SQL, feel free to inspect some [[sql/examples|examples]], and remember to come back later to contribute your own ones.

Once you are comfortable on SQL, or even before, feel free to nose around in the [[glossary/dbdev backlog|development backlog]] where we record and occasionally even explain all the actual development activities on our databases and maintenance tools.
